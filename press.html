About the game

Short. Chaos Convoy is a co-op third-person shooter for one to four players. You are a soldier robot of a rogue AI: strap on four guns, fire them all at once and fight through a generated facility, room by room.

Long. You are one of four soldier units serving the Overseer, a superintelligence that now runs humanity. Each deployment drops the squad from a hangar into a freshly generated industrial facility held by the human resistance, the Holdouts, and defended by the machines of a rival AI and their turrets. Every robot fires four weapons at once. Clear the facility room by room, take what falls, then return through the extraction lift, or don't make it back. Between runs you spend your loot in the hangar. The tone is dark satire, with no gore.

About DeltaX Games

DeltaX Games is an independent game studio. Fırat Sarıbaş (founder; game design and software) and Ali Barış Doğan (marketing and business development) are making Chaos Convoy together with freelance artists.
