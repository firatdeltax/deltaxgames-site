The game

Strap on four guns. Fire them all.

Chaos Convoy is a third-person co-op shooter for one to four players. You are a soldier robot in the service of a rogue superintelligence, the Overseer. Deploy from your hangar into a facility held by the human resistance, clear it room by room, then ride the lift home, or go down fighting.

01

All four guns, all at once

Every robot carries four weapons and they all fire together. The whole screen is bullets, and everything that dies pays you.

02

A squad of four, or just you

Play online with up to three friends. Solo is a complete way to play, not a lonely one.

03

A new facility every deployment

Each run is generated, so the rooms and routes change. Come back to the hangar, spend what you earned and go again.

04

A rogue AI with a sense of humour

The Overseer delivers monstrous orders politely, and it is funniest when it is being reassuring. Dark satire, no gore.

The world

Industry kept working after we left

Humanity's facilities now run under the Overseer. You will fight the Holdouts, the human resistance; the machines of a rival AI; and the automated turrets that hold the ground between them.

The Holdouts

The human resistance. Machine gunners, snipers, shield-bearers and rocketmen who would rather you left their facilities alone.

The Rival's machines

Troopers, commandos, robot dogs and heavy tanks, fielded by a second rogue AI that wants the same facilities you do.

The turrets

Double-gun and laser turrets that cover the routes between rooms, and do not care whose side you are on.
