Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

DeltaX Games decides what happens to the data described on this page. Questions, access requests and deletion requests all go to firat@deltaxgames.com.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no forms, and no scripts, fonts or images loaded from other sites.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when it serves a page, under its own privacy statement. We don't see or use that data.
  • If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and keep the conversation for as long as we need it for that.

Chaos Convoy: gameplay data

This part applies to test builds of Chaos Convoy and, later, the released game.

What we collect

Pseudonymous gameplay statistics: runs, rooms, kills, deaths, purchases, session length, co-op squad size, connection quality, frame times, hardware class, game settings and error messages.

What we don't collect

Your name, names you type in the game, chat, voice, join codes or Steam profile data. We don't add your IP address to the gameplay data. As with any online service, the providers listed below see your IP address when the game sends them data.

Your ID

If you play through Steam, your Steam ID is replaced by a one-way hash before it leaves your computer. That makes it a pseudonym, not anonymous: someone who knows your Steam ID and our hashing method could work out which data is yours. Without Steam, the game uses a random install ID created on your computer.

Why

To balance the game and fix bugs while we test it.

Who handles it

  • GameAnalytics shows us summary dashboards.
  • Cloudflare stores the raw data, in the EU jurisdiction.
  • Unity Relay and Vivox carry multiplayer sessions and voice chat. That traffic is the game itself, not our gameplay statistics.

How long we keep it

Raw data is kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. The game also keeps a copy of your last 30 sessions in its data folder on your own computer.

Your choice

  • Test builds share gameplay data by default, and tell you so the first time you start the game.
  • You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings → GAME → Share gameplay data. Nothing is sent while it is off, and anything recorded while it was off is never sent later.
  • Public builds will ask you first, and send nothing until you say yes.

Deleting your data

Email firat@deltaxgames.com with the install code shown in Settings → GAME. We will delete the matching data from both providers. You can also ask us what we hold about you or to correct it.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top.
