The idea

Mount four. Fire all four.

Your robot does not hold a weapon. It mounts one on each of its four hardpoints: two arms and two shoulders. Hold the trigger and all four fire together at one crosshair.

Mix any four. Put a Minigun next to a Shotgun, a Laser and a Grenade launcher, or mount four of the same gun. Your build is the set of guns that fire together.

Co-op

Bring three friends

Chaos Convoy is online co-op for 1 to 4 players. One player hosts. Friends join from a Steam invite or with a short join code.

  • Talk

    Built-in voice chat for the whole squad.

  • Ping

    Mark a target and everyone sees it.

  • Revive

    Pick up a squadmate who goes down.

  • Friendly fire

    On or off. It is the host's choice.

Every run

A facility that is never the same

Every run generates a new facility: new rooms, new routes, new enemies behind each door. You clear it one room at a time, then call the lift and hold the room until it arrives.

01

Read the door

A plate on every door shows what kind of room is waiting behind it.

02

Some rooms fight back

A ceiling collapses in the middle of a fight. A door locks behind you and a bomb starts counting. A crate is a trap.

03

Hold the lift

At the end the squad calls the lift and holds the room until it arrives. Then you ride it home.

04

Seven weapon types

With rare variants that carry extra mods. Guns you bring home stay in your armoury.

05

Bigger guns, new modules

Upgrade your hardpoints to carry heavier weapons, and equip modules such as a barrier dome, a shockwave or an attack drone.

06

Solo, offline, yours

Every room, every upgrade and every difficulty tier can be played alone, with no internet connection. Enemy numbers adjust to the size of your squad.

The hangar

Build your robot

Everyone starts with the same body. In the hangar you make it yours: helmet, chest, shoulder and knee plates, a screen face, and any bright colour you like.

Plates, a face and a colour

Swap the helmet, the chest, the shoulders and the knees. Pick the face your robot shows on its screen. Choose the colour your squad will know you by.

An armoury that grows

Guns you bring home stay in your armoury. Rare guns carry extra mods. Upgrade your hardpoints, mount something bigger, and deploy again.

Your employer

You work for the Overseer

Your squad was destroyed. The next one is ready. That is you again.

The Overseer

The Overseer is the artificial intelligence that built you. It gives the orders, and it comments on your work after every room. It is polite. It is not kind.

The Holdouts

The humans who still resist. Snipers, rocketmen and shield-bearers hold the halls.

The Rival's machines

Troopers and robot dogs, fielded by a rival intelligence.

The turrets

The Rival's guns, bolted down along the routes between rooms.

The facility

Down in the mine

Bore machines, ore carts and industrial halls, held room by room by people and machines who would rather you had stayed in the hangar.
